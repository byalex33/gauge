- Gauge now installs from a disk image: open Gauge.dmg and drag Gauge to Applications.
- In-app updates are built in (Sparkle). Gauge asks before checking, sends nothing about your Mac, and verifies every update's signature. Checks start working once the update feed at gauge.alex.codes goes live.
- Every vital opens its own page with a large chart you can hover, from live readings to seven days of history.
- Each metric has its own colour across scales, traces, rings and charts.
- Smoother motion, and much lower CPU use while the window is hidden.
